# DQClock Users Manual

Congratulations on your new clock! **DQClock** is your new friendly alarm clock and sleep trainer.

## Main Parts

./AlarmParts.png

1. The **screen** shows the current time.
2. The **LED Ring** will set the mood. During the day, it is green; when the alarm rings, it flashes red. You can configure the color and brightness at night.
3. The **speaker** will play the configured sounds at alarm time.
4. The **USB connector** is used to power the clock. 
5. The **touch sensor** on the top is used to stop or snooze the alarm and to see the information screen.
 
## Initial Setup

The first time you power up the clock, you need to set up its WiFi connection.


./FirstTime.png


Connect your cellphone or PC to the **dqsoft** WiFi network (no password is required) and use a browser to navigate to 192.168.4.1 (you may get a warning that a secure connection cannot be established; ignore it).

./WiFiConfig.png

Enter the WiFi name and Password for your WiFi connection.

The clock will restart when you press **Connect**.

## Configuration

You can change the clock's configuration by accessing it in a browser (again, you may get a warning that a secure connection cannot be established; ignore it). The clock IP address appears when it turns on; you can also view it by touching the top sensor for more than 5 seconds and releasing it.

./ClockConfig.png

The configurations available are:

* Alarm on weekends: selects whether the alarm will be active on Saturdays and Sundays.
* Wake Alarm: selects the wake time and sound. You can also select how many minutes the snooze will give.
* Sleep Alarm: selects the sleep time and sound.
* Night settings: selects the color and brightness of the LED ring during the night period (from 7 PM to 6 AM).

The clock will restart when you press **Apply**.

## Operation

The screen shows the current time and a pair of eyes. During the day, the eyes are open; during the night, they are closed.

./Hello.png

When the alarm time is reached, the alarm sounds through the speaker, the LED ring flashes red, and the eyes change. A short press (less than 5 seconds) on the touch sensor stops the alarm. A long (more than 5 seconds) press on the touch sensor activates the snooze feature.

When the alarm is off, a long press (more than 5 seconds) on the touch sensor brings up the information screen.
