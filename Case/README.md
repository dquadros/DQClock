# Alarm Case

The alarm case was printed on a 3D printer I just got. The project was done on OpenSCAD.

My lack of experience with 3D printer is probably evident. With more than 3 hours of printing per piece, I did not made many iterations to clean up mistakes.

Overall the pieces are massive. This makes them more robust, at the cost of more material. I should also have worked more on how to fix the pieces together.

As an alternative, you can use a square wooden box.
